# Give visitors a record to try

**Vinylytics already has its eye-catcher. Put a finished record sleeve in the first phone screen and let visitors change its ink.** Someone arriving from Instagram could understand what they would get before deciding whether to set it up on a computer. Inside the app, give the listening fingerprint the same treatment. It is already a record drawn from someone's listening habits, but that exact drawing disappears on the way to the shareable image. One launch issue comes first: on 19 November 2026 the address printed on a generated card opened a different product. These priorities come from inspecting both current local experiences and comparing published click, sharing and performance research. Vinylytics visitor analytics and field timings were unavailable, so the proposed effects need a local test.

## What the click and sharing evidence supports

| Question | What researchers or product teams measured | How it applies here |
|---|---|---|
| Will visitors discover the fifth example? | Around 1% of Notre Dame homepage visitors clicked a featured story. Of 28,928 featured-story clicks, 84% went to the first position. On another university property 8.8% clicked a feature and the first position received 40% of those clicks. Different sites and stories gave different results. [Original instrumentation](https://erikrunyon.com/2013/01/carousel-interaction-stats/) | This is historical carousel data. For the scrolling page, test fifth-chapter discovery directly. Show the strongest output early and measure comprehension as well as taps. |
| Can people tell what to press? | In a randomized desktop eyetracking study, 71 participants sought targets on nine interfaces. Weak visual cues increased time to locate the intended target by 22% on average. The study measured finding the target. [NN/g experiment](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/flat-ui-less-attention-cause-uncertainty/) | Make the next destination explicit with “Set up on your computer.” Named ink buttons with an obvious selected state teach the demo in one tap. |
| Does a playable demo lead somewhere? | Among roughly 5,000 B2B SaaS sites Navattic sampled, 18% offered a demo call to action. In its own top quartile of qualifying demos, 29% of **already-engaged** viewers clicked an external CTA. The vendor ranked cohorts by outbound click-through. [Report and methodology](https://www.navattic.com/report/state-of-the-interactive-product-demo-2026/) | Compare setup-guide visits per **all eligible landing sessions**. A recoloring toy can earn lots of taps while doing little for setup. |
| What makes people share a personal result? | Duolingo says its top 10% of XP earners generated over half of its 2020 year-review shares. Later adding a second opportunity to share a learner personality raised sharing, with no published effect size. Spotify reported over 630 million social shares across 2025 Wrapped and over 300 million engaged users. Those are whole-campaign totals. [Duolingo product team](https://blog.duolingo.com/year-in-review-behind-the-scenes/) · [Spotify earnings](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-02-10/spotify-q4-2025-earnings/) | Offer a personal pattern as well as a ranked achievement. A casual listener can have a distinctive record even with few minutes played. Neither source identifies a winning button or animation. |
| Which keepsakes matter after the reveal? | A study combining social-network analysis and interviews with 38 Brazilian Spotify users aged 18–33 found people saved screenshots, compared years and used results to start conversations. It identifies motivations in that group. [Authors' paper](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/240195/) | Ask separately what someone would save, send to one friend and post on a public Story. Those are three different product outcomes. |
| Does speed change outcomes? | Vodafone split paid traffic between visually equivalent pages, about 34,000 visits per variant per day. The optimized page moved largest contentful paint from 8.3 s to 5.7 s and produced 8% more sales. Multiple delivery changes shipped together. [Vodafone test](https://web.dev/case-studies/vodafone) | This supports testing faster delivery against an actual outcome. It supplies no sales or signup forecast for a lightweight self-hosted music app. |

## Seven changes worth testing, in order

| Priority | Change | Expected value and effort | First check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Repair the address on outgoing cards. Verify it on a phone after exporting a fresh PNG. | Launch prerequisite. Small code change once a destination is confirmed. | The printed address opens the intended public page, and the link preview identifies Vinylytics. |
| **2** | Put a playable sample sleeve in the launch hero. Show the finished art immediately, three named inks and one plainly labeled setup link. | Highest-value launch experiment. Small static prototype. | Can a visitor explain the product, change the ink and find setup unaided? |
| **3** | Add **Share this record** beside the Recap fingerprint. Carry the same grooves through the Story and the exported PNG. | Strongest app-specific idea. Moderate renderer work. | The downloaded groove pattern matches the tile. Track completed saves or share handoffs per fingerprint viewed. |
| **4** | Add a “See a share card” jump to the final chapter. Keep the user-paced tour and try portrait crops of the focal detail on phones. | Useful for visitors who want depth. Small image and layout changes. | Can a visitor reach the Cards chapter and read its controls without traversing all five or zooming? |
| **5** | Give **This week** a postcard. One tap opens a dated card with the weekly observation. Offer a plain stat and the existing roast as two choices. | A reason to return between annual recaps. Moderate, using the current composer. | Compare completed exports, and ask which version someone would send privately or post publicly. |
| **6** | Link a dated Recap fact to the plays behind it. Start with the repeated-track or late-night result and a **See that day** link. | Useful even for people who never share. Small, using the existing History route. | Does the right local day open, and can the listener return to the same highlight? |
| **7** | End a first successful import with one discovery. Pair the real new-play count with “First play in this export” and **Open that day**. | Makes an otherwise administrative wait pay off. Small if the existing data supplies the first play. | Time from completion to opening a listening result. A duplicate import should report that the library is up to date. |

**How the first screen could feel.** Keep the line “Your Spotify history, pressed as records.” Put one sleeve close enough to read its artist and dates at phone size. A visitor taps Cobalt and the same sleeve changes ink under their finger. The sample can render as an ordinary image before the controls start. One existing era image is **10,616 bytes**. All three existing sample cards total 34,570 bytes, while the five tour screenshots total 241,626 bytes on disk. These numbers establish feasibility for a prototype. A network trace would show the bytes that actually arrive before the first interaction. Raycast’s image tool demonstrates an editable result with export, and Plausible gives its sample dashboard and setup distinct entrances. [Raycast image tool](https://ray.so/) · [Plausible](https://plausible.io/) · [Existing media](../../vinylytics-site/public/media)

**Why the fingerprint deserves its own component.** Each ring is a weekday and each of its 24 segments an hour. The groove weight reflects minutes. The current Story identifies the fingerprint as “Yours alone,” while its PNG renderer receives artist artwork and a daily-minutes series. Pass the weekday/hour grid all the way through. Keep orientation constant, add one sentence identifying the actual peak window, and allow the final record to appear instantly with reduced motion. If listeners enjoy holding onto the image, try comparing two years with a shared intensity scale and matched date windows. A year-to-date record matched against an entire earlier year can give a misleading impression. [Fingerprint geometry](../src/components/recap/fingerprint.tsx) · [Story data](../src/lib/spotify/recap-slides.ts) · [Card export](../src/app/api/card/recap/route.ts)

**Why the weekly card has a hook.** The synthetic Shelf read “57% Saint Juniper. Other artists exist, we checked.” That is a complete message someone could send to a friend. A generic Share button on a chart leaves the listener to decide what the chart says. A dated card preserves the punchline and gives it context. Spotify also puts a distinctive weekly milestone next to a sharing action. The announcement documents the practice, with no published click rate. [Spotify Listening Stats](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-11-06/spotify-new-feature-listening-stats/)

## Where motion earns its place

| Desired feel | Working reference | Treatment for Vinylytics |
|---|---|---|
| **Tactile** | [Motion shared layout](https://motion.dev/docs/react-layout-animations) | Borrow the continuity: the same record grows from tile to preview with its grooves in place. Keep the existing shelf morph. In controlled experiments, carefully staged chart transitions improved perception on specific tasks, which supports motion when it helps someone follow an object. [Heer and Robertson](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/communication/HeerRobertson2007.pdf) |
| **Decisive** | [Base UI Tabs](https://base-ui.com/react/components/tabs) · [Cult UI Direction Aware Tabs](https://www.cult-ui.com/docs/components/direction-aware-tabs) | Use these selectors for the hero sample or a two-year comparison. The current tour already moves at the reader’s pace. If testing the earlier autoplay alternative again, add a visible stop/start control and explicit restart after keyboard focus enters. [W3C carousel pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/carousel/) |
| **Responsive** | [Motion performance guide](https://motion.dev/docs/performance) · [Chrome carousel guidance](https://web.dev/articles/carousel-best-practices) | Load the initial image from HTML and reserve its dimensions. Compare the new full-viewport animated Spiral against a still groove background on a midrange phone. Capture cold load, frame pacing during scroll and preference under reduced motion. On the composer, time both tap to visible response and final ink selection to a decoded PNG. |
| **Satisfying** | [Base UI Progress](https://base-ui.com/react/components/progress) · [Progress-bar experiment](https://chrisharrison.net/projects/progressbars/ProgBarHarrison.pdf) | The current import already reports actual saved work. In a 22-person experiment, a late pause made equal 5.5-second waits feel longer. Keep preparation indeterminate, acknowledge saved work promptly and give completion a destination. The meaningful payoff is discovering which track started the archive. |

The composer already has a record-and-tonearm loader, cancels obsolete requests, and prevents saving a stale PNG. Test a cold first open and several rapid ink selections on a phone before adding effects. If the dynamic import delays the initial response, show the small dialog shell earlier. If unused PNG renders dominate subsequent changes, coalesce rapid selections and time the final decoded file. Track responsiveness separately from network completion. For field comparisons the standard “good” thresholds at the 75th percentile are LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 milliseconds and CLS ≤0.1, split by mobile and desktop. No production timing baseline was collected for this review. [Composer](../src/components/sleeve/card-composer.tsx) · [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)

## Run the first test with Instagram listeners

Use synthetic data and a phone. Show people either the current launch hero or the card-first version and ask, **“A friend sent you this. Show me what you could make, then where you’d go to make your own.”** Record their first tap, whether they reach the right guide, how long it takes and what they think happens next. Alternate which version people see first. A few observed sessions can expose confusing labels. A quantitative claim about which version converts better needs enough independent visits and a preselected outcome.

On your personal Instagram, post **A**, a minutes-total poster, and **B**, the genuine listening fingerprint, using the same synthetic history, ink and format. Ask “Which would you put on your Story?” Use a follow-up question sticker or reply for “neither.” Ask separately which they would DM to a friend and which they would save for themselves. Your followers are a convenience sample, and a poll captures stated preference. In a follow-up session, hand listeners the app, observe which card they open and save, and check that the saved file contains what made them choose it. The weekly roast makes a good third candidate for a later round.

If traffic warrants a launch A/B test, use **setup-guide exits per eligible landing session** as the primary outcome and count intentional sample interactions separately from timer events. Reaching GitHub establishes setup intent. An observed successful install answers the next question. For in-app trials, keep share-sheet handoffs, file saves and confirmed public posts distinct. Simple counters or a moderated test with the demo history can answer these questions without recording artist names, search terms or anyone's listening history.
